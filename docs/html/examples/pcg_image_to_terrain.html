Image to terrain#

Lifts a single landscape image (a photo or an AI-painted perspective view) into a top-down height field, then continues with the terrain chain: a little stream-power erosion, a coloured mesh, a preview and a glb export.

Load it#

Drag example_workflows/pcg_image_to_terrain.json onto the ComfyUI canvas and choose your image in Load Image.

The workflow opens on ComfyUI's example.png, which is a cartoon figure, not a landscape: lifted, its flat sky becomes a wall at the back and the figure a spike. Pick a picture of ground running into the distance — a camera looking slightly down at a valley, hills or a coast, with a horizon — a photo or an AI-painted one.

Requirements

WhatDefault in the workflowNotes
Depth Anything V2DepthAnythingV2Preprocessor, depth_anything_v2_vitl.pthFrom the custom node pack comfyui_controlnet_aux; it downloads the weights automatically on the first run. This is the default depth route.
MoGe (optional)LoadMoGeModel with moge_2_vitl_normal.safetensors, MoGeInferenceComfyUI core nodes. Put the model in models/geometry_estimation/. Both nodes are muted in the saved workflow.

Two depth routes#

Depth Anything V2 (default). Its output is relative inverse depth: it is known only up to a scale and a shift. The Shift and FOV X values of HeightField from Depth are therefore guesses, and a wrong guess bends the terrain rather than just scaling it.

MoGe (more accurate). MoGe provides camera-space points, metric scale and a sky mask directly. To use it, put the model in models/geometry_estimation/, select the two greyed-out MoGe nodes and press Ctrl+M to unmute them. The wires are already connected: while the MoGe nodes are muted, the front end drops every input that comes from a muted node, so HeightField from Depth uses depth; once they are unmuted, moge_geometry takes priority.

Step by step#

  1. Load Image provides the picture; DepthAnythingV2Preprocessor (resolution 1024) estimates depth, shown by a Preview Image ("near = white").
  2. COP HeightField from Depth (PCG) (mode perspective, resolution 640, depth kind disparity, FOV X 60) projects the depth into a top-down height field. It also takes the original image (for colour) and, when unmuted, the MoGe geometry. Its mask layer marks which cells were actually measured; the hidden areas behind mountains are filled by push-pull and are guesses. The world_size output gives the size of the area.
  3. COP HeightField Stream Power Erosion (PCG) (30 iterations, erodibility 0.02, diffusion 0.01) erodes it lightly. Its world_size input is wired from step 2, so the scale matches automatically.
  4. COP HeightField to Mesh (PCG) (step 2, attributes Cd=albedo mask, world_size wired as well) builds the mesh with the image colours.
  5. Geometry Preview (PCG) (Studio look) renders the lifted terrain and Export Mesh (PCG) writes pcg/terrain_from_image.

Worth tweaking#

NodeParameterEffect
HeightField from Depthfov_x, shiftThe two guesses of the Depth Anything route. Adjust them when the terrain looks bent.
HeightField from DepthresolutionHeight-field size in pixels.
HeightField from DepthdequantizeOn by default. Depth Anything through comfyui_controlnet_aux arrives as an 8-bit image (256 levels); each level would lift to one arc on the ground, giving horizontal streaks and terraces in the far field. Dequantize smooths the map back to continuous values within its levels; it does nothing to float depth or MoGe.
Stream Poweriterations, erodibilityHow much erosion is added on top of the measured shape.
DepthAnythingV2Preprocessorckpt_name, resolutionSmaller models (vitb, vits) run faster.
